02

Dos and don'ts

✓Do

  • Only share prices, discounts, lineups and other details that DG has confirmed or given you.
  • Ask your team lead if you're unsure whether something is OK to post.
  • Keep messages short, friendly and personal.
  • If a customer asks something you don't know, don't guess. Tell them you'll check, then ask your team lead.
  • Share ideas, content and wins with the team.
  • Represent DG positively and respectfully, online and at events.

✕Don't

  • Promise anything on DG's behalf that you can't guarantee.
  • Say you're DG staff or management.
  • Spam or harass people.
  • Use misleading tactics to get a sale.
  • Slander DG or make negative comments about DG, online or in person.
  • Use your promoter status to pressure or take advantage of anyone.
  • Share screenshots of the promoter chat or leak unannounced info like lineups or prices.
